# Privacy Policy for Status Saver Pro

**Effective Date:** June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Status Saver Pro ("we," "our," or "the App") handles your information. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

Status Saver Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

* We do not require you to create an account.
* We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.
* All processing of WhatsApp statuses (images and videos) occurs locally on your device.
* No status files, personal information, or media are uploaded to our servers.

## 2. Permissions and Access

To provide its functionality, the App requires certain permissions:

### Storage Access

The App uses Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) to allow you to select the WhatsApp Status folder. This permission is used solely to display and save status media on your device.

### Media Access

The App requires access to photos and videos to save selected status media to your device's Gallery or Downloads folder.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party libraries:

* Glide – Image loading and caching.
* ExoPlayer (Media3) – Video playback.
* PhotoView – Zooming and image viewing features.

These libraries are used only to provide app functionality and do not collect personal information through our App.

## 4. Cookies and Local Storage

This App does not use cookies. However, certain third-party libraries may temporarily cache images or videos on your device to improve performance and user experience.
